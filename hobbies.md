# Fun and hobbies

- [Codingame](https://codingame.com) - if you bored of regular competetive programming, codingame hosts multiplayer game bot challenges 2-3 times a year. It is more complex format but still beginners friendly. Ultimate Tic Tac Toe, Tron game and a lot of original games.
- [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com) - a programming puzzle every day in December, fun to solve together with classmates.
- [Lichess](https://lichess.org) - free and open-source chess site with puzzles and no ads.
